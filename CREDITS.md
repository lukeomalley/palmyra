# Asset credits

Wallpapers are resized and center-cropped to 1920x1080. Original photos 00-09
use JPEG quality 85. Owner-selected additions 10-14 use quality 92, 4:4:4 chroma
and sequential encoding, resized from high-resolution source downloads.
The first image is the default. Each photo also has a derived, lossless 240x135
blur PNG for the focus pill. The default JPEG uses lossless sequential encoding.
Brand names and marks belong to their owners.
